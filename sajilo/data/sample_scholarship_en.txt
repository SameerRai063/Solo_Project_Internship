GREEN VALLEY COLLEGE: MERIT SCHOLARSHIP 2026/27 (sample, fictional)

Green Valley College invites applications for the Merit Scholarship for the 2026/27 academic year.

Eligibility: Applicants must have scored at least 3.4 GPA in the last completed year and must be enrolled as full-time students.

Required documents: (a) completed application form, (b) latest transcript, (c) citizenship certificate copy, (d) one recommendation letter from a faculty member.

Deadline: Applications must be submitted to the Student Welfare Office by 15 December 2026, 4:00 PM. Late applications will not be considered.

Conditions: The scholarship covers 50% of tuition fees. Recipients must maintain a GPA of 3.2 or above in each semester; otherwise the scholarship will be cancelled and the amount received in that semester must be repaid. Recipients cannot hold any other scholarship at the same time.

Results will be published on the college notice board in January.
